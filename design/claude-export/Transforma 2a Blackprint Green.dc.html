transforma · approved visual direction
Blackprint, green
The signed-off direction, twelve mobile screens and the desktop adaptation. Ink ground, 2px rules, zero radius, green reserved for the primary action and for anything the trainer authored. Appearance is a client setting: ink is the default, paper is available, and the in-session screen stays ink in both so it reads at arm's length in gym lighting.
Accent
Fills use #0a6b43 with white type (5.9:1). #3ecb8e is the accent for text on ink; #0a6b43 for text on the light ground.

Accent
Fills #0a6b43 with white type. #3ecb8e for accent text on ink, #0a6b43 for accent text on paper, #10a86a for data marks only.
Structure
2px rules, zero radius, 2px gaps between tiles. Neutral-900 is the only card fill on ink; surface is the only one on paper.
Type
Archivo throughout. 36px screen titles, 15px body, 11px uppercase labels at .12em, tabular numerals for all logged values.
Coach rules
Plans and schedules are trainer-authored and read-only to the client, days off are attributed and dated, extra sessions log separately, and the coach sees only what the client shared.
