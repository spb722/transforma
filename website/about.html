About transforma

transforma wasn't built to be a product for everyone. It grew out of fifteen years of actually lifting, and it exists for two specific people: someone training seriously, and the trainer coaching them.

I've been lifting for fifteen years, and in that time I've tried most of the fitness apps out there. Nearly all of them are built for a generic user just getting started — not for someone chasing a real number in a real program. I wanted something built around how people who actually train want to log a session, not around a subscription funnel.

What turned it from "an app for me" into something worth building properly was a close friend, Akhil. He's starting his own gym, and alongside it he coaches clients both in person and online. He needed a way to assign workouts, see what his clients were actually doing, and keep track of all of it — while managing the gym's construction at the same time. He didn't have the bandwidth to build that himself, so I built it for him.

That's why transforma isn't just a personal tracker. Alongside the logging, body-weight tracking and guided workout flow built for training yourself, it has a full trainer–client coaching system: a coach assigns and edits workout and diet plans, a client logs against them without being able to quietly rewrite what was assigned, check-ins, progress and fees are tracked in one place, and a passkey-based invitation connects a client to their trainer without either of them handing over a password.

Everything else grew from actually using it: the weight pre-fills, the rest timer, sync across devices, 12 languages, a muscle map, importers for FitNotes, Strong, Hevy and Apple Health, and a native Android app you can install from this site.

It's open-sourced under the AGPL. The license is the point, not a footnote: your training data should be yours, the software that holds it should be inspectable, and nobody — including me — should be able to take a closed, paid version of it away from the people using it.

— Sachin

Principles

  • Your data is yours. Local-first, one-file backups, no lock-in — leaving transforma must always be one export away.
  • No telemetry, ever. If a feature needs analytics to justify itself, it doesn't ship.
  • Dependency-light. The backend is one Node file with a single dependency; the frontend is React, a router and a store. Small enough to actually audit.
  • Open source that stays open. AGPL-3.0, so every fork and every hosted version owes its code back to everyone.
